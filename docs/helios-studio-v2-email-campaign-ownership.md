# Email campaign ownership

EmailCampaign has nullable stored workspace ownership with a restricting foreign key. New manual, Blog-derived and Newsletter delivery campaigns record their company explicitly. Historical campaigns are not assigned from their creator's current account. The additive SQL preserves history and old inserts; verified mapping remains required.

Campaign history, draft load/update/delete and scheduling mutations are scoped. Unknown/foreign draft IDs fail rather than silently creating a replacement. Recipient and test-profile queries require client workspace membership; group selection also requires group ownership. Delivery resolves stored campaign ownership and checks client membership before preference tokens or provider use. Legacy null ownership resolves only with tenant mode off and one workspace. Newsletter retries verify the delivery campaign belongs to the approved workspace. Dashboard newsletter/email attention, counts, schedules and delivery metrics use stored ownership rather than creator membership.

Email campaign administration remains single-company while shared sender, consent, webhook and image paths are incomplete. The Resend adapter, webhook handlers, token layer and bounce preference writers are unchanged. In particular, webhook/bounce association still contains creator-derived ownership and requires a separately reviewed conversion with regression evidence before multi-company activation. This patch only changes campaign orchestration and ownership predicates; no provider configuration or real recipient is exercised.

Executable tests run actual orchestration with mocked preferences/provider/database services and verify an account move cannot change campaign context, foreign recipients receive no tokens/messages, and foreign scheduled IDs cannot be changed. SQL tests preserve old campaign text/writes. These do not establish real delivery or concurrent lease/cancellation correctness. Historical mapping, protected webhook ownership, sender configuration, approval/parity and browser tests remain release gates.
