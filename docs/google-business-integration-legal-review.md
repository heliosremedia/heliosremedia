# Google Business Profile integration legal review

These are proposed additions for Jake and legal counsel to review. They are not automatically inserted into or published through the managed Privacy Policy or Terms of Service.

## Privacy Policy addition requiring approval

### Google Business Profile integration

Authorized Helios administrators may connect the company’s Google Business Profile to Helios Studio. When connected, Helios Studio accesses the selected business account and location identifiers and may retrieve reviewer names, reviewer profile images when provided by Google, star ratings, review text, review creation and update timestamps, business replies, and synchronization status. This information is used to privately review and curate customer reviews for possible inclusion in the existing Featured Google Reviews presentation. Imported reviews are not automatically published.

Google authorization credentials are restricted to authorized administrative systems and are not displayed publicly. Refresh credentials are encrypted at rest. Administrative users with owner or administrator access may manage the connection and synchronized review records.

Integration records are retained while the connection is active and as needed to operate the curated review feature. Disconnecting Google removes the stored Google authorization and imported source records, but does not automatically remove a Featured Google Review that was separately curated and published. To request deletion of information associated with the integration, contact jake@heliosrealestatemedia.com. Helios will verify the request before acting. Google access may also be revoked through the Google Account permissions page.

## Terms of Service addition requiring approval

### Connected Google services

Helios Studio may allow authorized company administrators to connect a Google Business Profile for private review synchronization and curation. The administrator connecting the account represents that they are authorized to manage the selected business profile. Google services remain subject to Google’s own terms and availability. Helios does not represent that this integration is sponsored, certified, or endorsed by Google. Imported content remains subject to administrative review and is not automatically published.
